\section{The privileged direction is causally deployed}
\label{sec:causality}

If the recovered scalar were only a descriptive correlate, enforcing it would have no reason to help
and setting it no reason to steer. Three interventions say otherwise, each against matched controls
that differ only in \emph{where} they push, never in how hard --- a distinction that matters, since
the obvious norm-matched null controls nothing (Appendix~\ref{sec:causal-detail}).

Evaluated on decoded physical energy rather than pixels, projecting the latent back toward its level
set during imagination reduces drift by \textbf{55--76\%} at $H=190$ on 512 trajectories from a
disjoint generator seed, with the scalar and coordinate frame frozen --- and \textbf{0 of 60}
magnitude-matched random directions and \textbf{0 of 15} equal-norm tangent directions beat it.
Setting the scalar to an independent donor trajectory's value moves the imagined world's \emph{true
decoded} energy toward that trajectory's \emph{true} energy (rank correlation
$\mathbf{0.802}$--$\mathbf{0.914}$, \textbf{0 of 75} controls), with a preregistered offset sweep
monotone in both directions on every model. Repeating that interchange \textbf{50 steps into an
autonomous rollout} --- on a state the model reached by itself, not one the encoder produced --- costs
little ($0.762$--$0.849$ against $0.803$--$0.924$ at depth 0, 0 of 13 controls), which a dormant
pathway would not survive \citep{makelov2024illusion}.

The same pipeline refuses when there is nothing to find. On matched dissipative models, where no
nontrivial first integral exists, it returns a direction indistinguishable from random: 24 of 60
random draws beat it, and its operator statistic sits at $3.4$--$3.7\times10^{-1}$ --- the range of
an untrained network, $39\times$ worse than any conservative model, with no overlap.
Appendix~\ref{sec:causal-detail} gives protocols and per-model numbers.
