\section{Where the phenomenon stops}
\label{sec:boundaries}

Both boundaries below are measurements with matched controls rather than caveats, and each
constrains what produces conservation.

\paragraph{The invariant is bounded to the training distribution.}
We generate trajectories whose energies lie outside the training band, holding the integrator,
renderer and rejection rule fixed. The frozen $C$ collapses there, $|\rho|_E$ falling from
$0.93$--$0.97$ to $0.03$--$0.31$.

Refitting the entire pipeline on those latents separates two explanations. Identification recovers
immediately --- $|\rho|_E$ of $0.92$--$0.96$, and a freely fitted probe reaches $0.999$ --- so the
information is present and the polynomial family can express it. What does not recover is
conservation: $\rho_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is $55$--$267\times$ worse than in-distribution. There is no
well-conserved quantity to find outside the training band, which is why a scalar fitted where
conservation held has nothing to lock onto.

The model's \emph{representation} generalises out of distribution. Its \emph{conservation} does not.

\paragraph{The phenomenon is architecture-dependent.}
We repeat the full pipeline on a deliberately different world model: a deterministic convolutional
GRU autoencoder with no stochastic latent, no KL term and no unimix, its recurrent state matched at
512 so the extraction operates on a latent of the same dimensionality, and its transition trained by
an open-loop rollout term. The analysis code runs unchanged.

Identification transfers on 2 of 3 models ($|\rho|_E$ 0.97, 0.89, 0.19). Conservation transfers on
none: $\rho_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is $1.75$--$9.02$ against the RSSM's ${\sim}7\times10^{-3}$. Training the
transition seven times longer did not close the gap.

We are explicit about what this comparison cannot settle. An open-loop reconstruction term and a
prior--posterior KL are different losses, not the same loss at different strengths, so the residual
gap narrows toward the architecture without isolating it. What the comparison does establish is that
conservation is not an automatic consequence of training a pixel world model whose transition rolls
out accurately, and the supportable scope of every claim above is RSSM-like models, in distribution.
