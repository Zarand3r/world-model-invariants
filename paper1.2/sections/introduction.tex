\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A world model trained on simulated video is trained on the output of an integrator. We show it learns
the integrator.

On a DreamerV3 world model trained only on pendulum video, a label-free search recovers a conserved
scalar --- and that scalar is not textbook energy but the \emph{shadow} Hamiltonian of the
simulator's numerical scheme, $\tilde H = H + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}mg\tfrac{l}{2}\dot\theta\sin\theta$
\citep{hairer2006geometric}. The correction coefficient is a property of the discretisation, not of
pendulums, so it makes a parameter-free prediction: vary the simulator's timestep and the recovered
coefficient must follow. It does. Across a $4\times$ range of $\Delta t$ the recovered coefficient
tracks the integrator's prediction with slope $2.484 \pm 0.058$ against a predicted $2.500$, the
minimum landing exactly on prediction for 10 of 12 models and a wrong-sign control beaten 12 of 12.

\paragraph{Why this matters for interpretability.}
The immediate consequence is that a probe can be perfect and still wrong. We fit a polynomial readout
to \emph{true} physical energy; it reaches $|\rho| = 0.9999$, as good as a probe can be. Yet it is
$6.7\times$ less preserved by the model's own transition than the label-free scalar, and enforcing it
during imagination makes the model's physics \emph{worse}, while the label-free scalar improves it by
42\%. Nothing is wrong with the probe. The probe is fitted to a quantity the generating process does
not conserve --- and no amount of probe hygiene \citep{hewitt2019control,belinkov2022probing} detects
that, because probe hygiene tests the probe.

\paragraph{Measuring the operator instead.}
The discriminating measurement is not on the representation but on the transition: apply one
autonomous step at a real encoded state and ask how much a candidate scalar moves
(Equation~\ref{eq:rhoobs}). This single statistic separates trained from untrained models
($74\times$), conservative from dissipative ($39\times$, no overlap), in-distribution from out
($55$--$267\times$), one architecture from another ($767\times$), and a label-free scalar from a
supervised probe of the same quantity ($6.7\times$). On every one of those contrasts a probe reports
success. The dissociation is directional in each case we can construct: decodability is retained
where dynamical structure fails.

\paragraph{The quantity the operator selects is used by the model.}
Projecting the latent back toward its level set during imagination reduces drift in \emph{decoded
physical energy} by 55--76\% at long horizon on trajectories the method never saw, with 0 of 60
magnitude-matched random directions and 0 of 15 equal-norm tangent directions beating it. Setting the
scalar to an independent trajectory's value moves the imagined world's true energy toward that
trajectory's (rank correlation $0.80$--$0.91$, 0 of 75 controls). The same interchange applied 50
steps into an autonomous rollout --- on a state the model reached by itself --- works nearly as well,
which a dormant pathway would not \citep{makelov2024illusion}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(i) \textbf{World models learn their simulator's numerical scheme}: the recovered invariant is the
integrator's shadow Hamiltonian, and its coefficient tracks the simulator timestep across a $4\times$
range with a parameter-free slope of $2.484 \pm 0.058$ against $2.500$. (ii) \textbf{A demonstration
that probing over-reports, with a decisive control and a mechanism}: a supervised probe of the true
quantity, at $|\rho| = 0.9999$, identifies a direction the dynamics do not use, because it is fitted
to a quantity the generating process does not conserve; the dissociation appears on five independent
axes and is directional in all of them. (iii) \textbf{An operator statistic that discriminates where
probes cannot}, with magnitude-matched rather than norm-matched controls --- the norm-matched null
used in prior work on this system controls nothing, because the correction is scale-invariant in the
constraint. (iv) \textbf{Causal evidence and stated boundaries}: the selected direction is deployed,
including 50 steps into autonomous imagination, and the correction confers no benefit to a planner,
for a reason we measure.
