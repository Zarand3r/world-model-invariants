\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Probing is how the field asks whether a model represents a quantity: fit a readout, report the
accuracy. On a DreamerV3 world model trained only on pendulum video, a polynomial readout fitted to
the pendulum's \emph{true} energy reaches $|\rho|_E = 0.9999$. By the usual reading, the model
represents energy about as well as anything can be represented.

It does not use it. The direction that readout identifies is one the model's own transition fails to
preserve, and enforcing it during imagination makes the model's physics \emph{worse}. A label-free
search that tracks energy less well --- $|\rho|_E = 0.973$ --- but is $6.7\times$ better preserved by
the transition improves it by 42\%. Whether a latent \emph{encodes} a quantity and whether the
dynamics \emph{preserve} it are different properties, and no amount of probe hygiene closes the gap,
because nothing is wrong with the probe.

We measure the second property directly with a statistic on the transition operator, show the two
dissociate on six axes, and show the quantity the operator selects is causally deployed rather than
descriptive: enforcing it cuts drift in decoded physical energy by 55--76\% on unseen trajectories,
setting it steers the imagined world's true energy, and the effect survives 50 steps into autonomous
imagination.

Why should the two come apart at all, when the model was trained on pendulums? A candidate answer is
that the training data does not conserve textbook energy either: it comes from a symplectic
integrator, which conserves a nearby shadow $\tilde H = H + \mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$
\citep{hairer2006geometric}. On ground truth that predicted coefficient is conserved $591\times$
better than $H$ with nothing fitted, and correcting the $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$ term flips the
intervention from harmful to repairing on every model. We present this as an account of the data and
are explicit in Section~\ref{sec:mechanism} that the corresponding claim about the \emph{model} is
one our instruments cannot decide.

\paragraph{Why this matters for interpretability.}
The immediate consequence is that a probe can be perfect and still wrong. We fit a polynomial readout
to \emph{true} physical energy; it reaches $|\rho| = 0.9999$, as good as a probe can be. Yet it is
$6.7\times$ less preserved by the model's own transition than the label-free scalar, and enforcing it
during imagination makes the model's physics \emph{worse}, while the label-free scalar improves it by
42\%. Nothing is wrong with the probe. The probe is fitted to a quantity the generating process does
not conserve --- and no amount of probe hygiene \citep{hewitt2019control,belinkov2022probing} detects
that, because probe hygiene tests the probe.

\paragraph{Measuring the operator instead.}
The discriminating measurement is not on the representation but on the transition: apply one
autonomous step at a real encoded state and ask how much a candidate scalar moves
(Equation~\ref{eq:rhoobs}). This single statistic separates trained from untrained models
($74\times$), conservative from dissipative ($39\times$, no overlap), in-distribution from out
($55$--$267\times$), one architecture from another ($767\times$), and a label-free scalar from a
supervised probe of the same quantity ($6.7\times$). On every one of those contrasts a probe reports
success. The dissociation is directional in each case we can construct: decodability is retained
where dynamical structure fails.

\paragraph{The quantity the operator selects is used by the model.}
Projecting the latent back toward its level set during imagination reduces drift in \emph{decoded
physical energy} by 55--76\% at long horizon on trajectories the method never saw, with 0 of 60
magnitude-matched random directions and 0 of 15 equal-norm tangent directions beating it. Setting the
scalar to an independent trajectory's value moves the imagined world's true energy toward that
trajectory's (rank correlation $0.80$--$0.91$, 0 of 75 controls). The same interchange applied 50
steps into an autonomous rollout --- on a state the model reached by itself --- works nearly as well,
which a dormant pathway would not \citep{makelov2024illusion}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(i) \textbf{A demonstration that probing over-reports, with a decisive control}: a supervised probe of
the true quantity, at $|\rho| = 0.9999$, identifies a direction the dynamics do not use and that harms
the model when enforced; the dissociation appears on six axes and is directional in all of them, the
cleanest holding the data fixed while changing the architecture ($767\times$).
(ii) \textbf{An operator statistic that discriminates where probes cannot}, with magnitude-matched
rather than norm-matched controls --- the norm-matched null used in prior work on this system
controls nothing, because the correction is scale-invariant in the constraint.
(iii) \textbf{Causal evidence}: the selected direction is deployed, on 512 unseen trajectories and 50
steps into autonomous imagination, against magnitude-matched nulls that fail 0 of 60 and 0 of 75.
(iv) \textbf{An account of the gap, with its evidential status separated}: the shadow Hamiltonian of
the generating integrator explains why the perfect probe is fitted to the wrong target, verified on
ground truth; we report in full why the parallel claim about the model resists measurement here.
(v) \textbf{Stated boundaries}: the correction confers no benefit to a planner and does not select
checkpoints better than validation loss, for reasons we measure.
