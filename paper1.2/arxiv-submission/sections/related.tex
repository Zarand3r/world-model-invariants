\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{What probes establish.}
Probing shows a variable is recoverable, not that the computation uses it \citep{alain2017probing}.
Control tasks \citep{hewitt2019control} and later critiques \citep{belinkov2022probing} address the
confound between what the representation stores and what the probe can learn. Ours is a different
failure: the probe in Section~\ref{sec:dissociation} is fitted to the true quantity and reaches
$0.9999$, so no control-task correction applies, and it still identifies a direction the dynamics do
not use. Work on emergent world representations goes beyond probing by intervening
\citep{li2023othello,nanda2023othello}, the logic ours follows; \citet{makelov2024illusion} show such
interventions can succeed through pathways the model does not normally use, which our depth-50
interchange answers.

\paragraph{Discovering conserved quantities.}
ConservNet minimises a noise-regularised within-group variance \citep{ha2021conservnet}, of which our
invariance ratio is the closed-form Rayleigh-quotient analogue; AI Poincar\'e counts conserved
quantities from trajectory manifolds \citep{liu2021aipoincare}. ConCerNet learns a conservation law
then projects the learned dynamics onto it \citep{zhang2023concernet}, FINDE finds first integrals and
preserves them by projection \citep{matsubara2023finde}, and Noether Networks meta-learn a conserved
quantity and apply it at test time, including on pixel pendulums \citep{alet2021noether}. All
\emph{impose or co-train} conservation. We ask whether a model trained with no such term already has
a scalar its transition preserves, measured on the frozen transition rather than built in.

\paragraph{Structure by design, and whether it is what helps.}
Hamiltonian and Lagrangian networks build conservation into an architecture
\citep{greydanus2019hnn,cranmer2020lnn}. \citet{gruver2022deconstructing} find HNN generalisation
comes from modelling acceleration and coordinate choice \emph{rather than} from symplectic structure,
which licenses the objection that our projection is a generic regulariser. The magnitude-matched null
answers it: with step size fixed, 0 of 60 random and 0 of 15 equal-norm tangent directions match the
recovered one, and on dissipative models the pipeline returns a direction indistinguishable from
random.

\paragraph{Drift, and evaluating physics in world models.}
That accurate one-step models drift over long rollouts is documented across fields; in machine-learned
force fields, force accuracy and simulation stability decouple sharply \citep{fu2023forces}. Projection
onto a level set is a standard structure-preserving device \citep{hairer2006geometric2}, and our
correction applies it to a \emph{discovered} rather than a given invariant, in a learned latent. World
models are otherwise evaluated by prediction quality or control performance
\citep{hafner2023dreamerv3,ha2018world,schrittwieser2020muzero}, and physics benchmarks evaluate
generated output --- neither reads the internal state. The disentanglement literature
\citep{locatello2019challenging} documents how readily an appealing structural claim about
representations survives without the control that would falsify it.
