\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{What probes establish.}
Probing shows a variable is recoverable from a representation, not that the model's computation uses
it \citep{alain2017probing}. Control tasks \citep{hewitt2019control} and later critiques
\citep{belinkov2022probing} address the confound between what the representation stores and what the
probe can learn. Our result is a different failure: the probe in Section~\ref{sec:dissociation} is
fitted to the true physical quantity and reaches $0.9999$, so no control-task correction applies to
it, and it still identifies a direction the dynamics do not use. Work on emergent world
representations goes beyond probing by intervening \citep{li2023othello,nanda2023othello}, which is
the logic our interventions follow; \citet{makelov2024illusion} show such interventions can succeed
through pathways the model does not normally use, which is the objection our depth-50 interchange is
designed to answer.

\paragraph{Discovering conserved quantities.}
A substantial literature finds conservation laws from data. ConservNet minimises a noise-regularised
within-group variance \citep{ha2021conservnet}, of which our invariance ratio is the closed-form
Rayleigh-quotient analogue; AI Poincar\'e counts conserved quantities from trajectory manifolds
\citep{liu2021aipoincare}. ConCerNet learns a conservation law and then projects the learned dynamics
onto it \citep{zhang2023concernet}, and FINDE finds first integrals and preserves them by projection
\citep{matsubara2023finde}; Noether Networks meta-learn a conserved quantity and apply it at test
time, including on pixel pendulums \citep{alet2021noether}. All of these \emph{impose or co-train}
conservation. We ask a different question: whether a model trained with no such term has a scalar its
transition already preserves, and we measure that on the frozen transition rather than building it in.

\paragraph{Structure by design, and whether it is what helps.}
Hamiltonian and Lagrangian networks build conservation into an architecture
\citep{greydanus2019hnn,cranmer2020lnn}. \citet{gruver2022deconstructing} find that HNN generalisation
comes from modelling acceleration and coordinate choice \emph{rather than} from symplectic structure
or energy conservation --- which licenses the objection that our projection is a generic regulariser.
Our magnitude-matched null answers it directly: with step size fixed, 0 of 60 random directions and
0 of 15 equal-norm tangent directions match the recovered one, and on dissipative models the same
pipeline returns a direction indistinguishable from random.

\paragraph{Drift in learned dynamics.}
That accurate one-step models drift over long rollouts is documented across fields. In machine-learned
force fields, force accuracy and simulation stability decouple sharply \citep{fu2023forces}. In
numerical analysis, projection onto a level set is a standard structure-preserving device
\citep{hairer2006geometric2}, and our correction is that device applied to a \emph{discovered} rather
than a given invariant, in a learned latent rather than the physical state.

\paragraph{Evaluating physics in world models.}
World models are normally evaluated by prediction quality or control performance
\citep{hafner2023dreamerv3,ha2018world,schrittwieser2020muzero}, and physics benchmarks evaluate
generated output. Neither reads the model's internal state. The disentanglement literature
\citep{locatello2019challenging} documents how readily an appealing structural claim about
representations survives without the control that would falsify it.
