\begin{abstract}
A world model trained only on pendulum video learns a conserved quantity --- but not the one physics
prescribes. It learns the \emph{shadow} Hamiltonian of the simulator's integrator,
$\tilde H = H + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}mg\tfrac{l}{2}\dot\theta\sin\theta$, whose correction coefficient
is a property of the discretisation rather than of pendulums. We test that directly by varying the
simulator's timestep: across a $4\times$ range, the coefficient the model recovers tracks the
integrator's prediction with a slope of $2.484 \pm 0.058$ against a parameter-free $2.500$, with the
minimum landing exactly on prediction for 10 of 12 models and a wrong-sign control beaten 12 of 12.
As the timestep coarsens, textbook energy becomes progressively worse conserved by the model
($\Delta t^{1.41}$) while the shadow quantity stays at the model's own floor, so the gap between them
grows from $1.03\times$ to $13.5\times$.

This has a sharp consequence for interpretability. A polynomial readout fitted to \emph{true} physical
energy reaches a correlation of $0.9999$ --- yet it is $6.7\times$ less preserved by the model's own
transition than a label-free scalar, and enforcing it during imagination makes the model's physics
worse, while the label-free scalar improves it by 42\%. The perfect probe is fitted to a quantity the
generating process does not conserve, and no amount of probe hygiene detects this, because nothing is
wrong with the probe. Whether a latent encodes a quantity and whether the dynamics preserve it are
different properties, and they dissociate on five axes --- untrained models, out-of-distribution
states, a second architecture, an untrained transition, and actuation --- always with decodability
surviving where dynamical structure fails. A statistic on the transition operator separates them in
every case, and the quantity it selects is causally deployed: enforcing it cuts drift in decoded
physical energy by 55--76\% on unseen trajectories, with 0 of 60 magnitude-matched random directions
beating it, and the effect survives an interchange 50 steps into autonomous imagination. We also
report where it stops: the correction confers no benefit to a planner, and we measure why.
\end{abstract}
