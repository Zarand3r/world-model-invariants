\section{Conclusion}

A DreamerV3 trained only on pendulum video learns an energy-like latent quantity that its own
dynamics approximately preserve on observation-conditioned trajectories. Correlation alone does not
establish this: randomly initialized models also admit strongly energy-correlated readouts. The
stronger evidence comes from matched damping and intervention. The invariant disappears when the
training dynamics are dissipative, and correcting its drift during autonomous imagination improves
prediction in every conservative model.

The result exposes a specific gap between learning physical structure and respecting it during
rollout. A world model can encode a useful dynamical constraint yet violate that constraint when it
predicts autonomously. Whether this phenomenon survives in systems with actions, contacts, and
multiple interacting objects is the main open question.
