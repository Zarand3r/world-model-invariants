\section{Conclusion}

A DreamerV3 trained only to predict pendulum video holds a quantity that its own dynamics conserve,
that disappears when we remove the physics from the training data, and that improves its rollouts
when we enforce it. The evidence is the separation from three controls, not the correlation itself,
because an untrained model clears the correlation threshold we had registered.

The result suggests a design question we cannot answer yet. If the model carries a physical quantity
and lets it drift, and if correcting that drift from outside improves prediction, then we might build a world model
that preserves it during imagination instead. Whether that helps at a scale where the
physics is not a single pendulum is the experiment we would run next.
