\section{Conclusion}

A DreamerV3 trained only on pendulum video develops a reproducible energy-like invariant. Energy
correlation alone does not establish that result: randomly initialized models can also produce high
correlations. The stronger evidence comes from three independent tests. The recovered scalar is
approximately conserved along observation-conditioned trajectories in conservative models; the same
search finds no approximately conserved degree-4 scalar in matched damped models; and correcting the
scalar's drift during autonomous imagination improves prediction. The intervention lowers rollout
error by 2.9--3.5\% on all three trained conservative models, although it beats matched random
constraints on only two of the three.

The result also exposes a gap between representation and transition. The model learns a scalar that
behaves like an invariant on real trajectories but does not preserve it perfectly when it imagines
forward. A natural next question is whether world-model transitions can preserve such learned
structure by construction, and whether the same effect survives in systems with actions, contacts,
and multiple interacting objects.
